# Voice Builder

Capture a personal writing voice and reproduce it reliably.

## Phase 1 — Build the voice profile (from 3+ writing samples)
Analyze and document:
- **Lexicon** — favorite words/phrases, jargon level, words they never use.
- **Syntax** — sentence length distribution, punctuation habits, fragments?, questions?, profanity?
- **Tone sliders** — formal↔casual, serious↔humorous, teaching↔opinionated, reserved↔intense.
- **Structure** — how they open/close, use of stories, lists, formatting, emoji/sig-off quirks.
- **Perspective** — POV, how they cite evidence, how they handle disagreement.

## Phase 2 — Write in that voice
1. Draft using the profile; 2. Self-check against 5 most distinctive markers; 3. Fix misses.

## Output
`voice-profile.md` (reusable) + the new content + checklist confirming each marker.
