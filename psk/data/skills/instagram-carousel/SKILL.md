# Instagram Carousel

Carousels structured for swipe-through and saves.

## Slide architecture (6-10 slides)
1. **Cover** — big hook promise ("5 hooks that beat the algorithm in 2026"), high contrast, readable at feed size.
2. **Slide 2** — re-hook: why it matters / who it's for ("Save this for your next launch").
3. **Slides 3..N-1** — one point per slide: bold headline line + 2-4 supporting lines; visual consistency (same layout system, accent per point); progress feels (3/10).
4. **Penultimate** — recap or the unexpected final tip.
5. **Final** — CTA: save/share/follow; clear next step.

## Copy rules
- Headline per slide <= 8 words; body <= 25 words; design for thumb-speed reading.
- Teach or entertain — every slide must justify existing.

## Output
Slide-by-slide table (slide #, headline, body text, visual note) + caption (hook + value + hashtags) + cover design prompt.
