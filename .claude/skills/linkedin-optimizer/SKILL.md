# LinkedIn Optimizer

Posts engineered for LinkedIn's feed and audience.

## Structure
1. **Hook line (1st line)** — works standalone above "see more": bold claim, result number, or tension ("I fired my best client.")
2. **Whitespace pacing** — 1-2 sentence lines; blank line between thoughts; vertical scan rhythm.
3. **Body patterns** — story→lesson, contrarian take→reasoning, list with bolded lead-ins, before/after.
4. **Close** — single reflective line or question (real question, not bait).
5. **Hashtags** — 0-3 niche tags; no walls of tags.

## Dwell-time tactics
- First-hour engagement plan: reply to every comment with substance; 2-3 comments on others' posts pre-publish.
- Native content only in post body; links in first comment.
- Optimal mixes: text + native document (carousel PDF) + occasional text-only.

## Output
Formatted post (ready to paste) + 2 hook variants + first-hour engagement checklist.
