# Hook Generator

Hooks that stop the scroll and pull the audience in.

## Proven frameworks (use at least 5 per request)
1. **Contrarian** — challenge a common belief: "SEO isn't dead. Your strategy is."
2. **Curiosity gap** — tease the payoff, withhold the how.
3. **Specific result** — numbers + timeframe: "0 to 34k followers in 90 days."
4. **Story in medias res** — drop into the moment of tension.
5. **Enemy/villain** — name what the audience already hates.
6. **Direct call-out** — speaks to the exact person: "Solo founders doing their own marketing—"
7. **Bold promise + proof** — claim then immediately validate.
8. **Question** — one the audience is already asking themselves.

## Method
1. Extract: audience, topic, platform (X hook ≠ reel hook ≠ email subject), desired emotion.
2. Generate 15 hooks across frameworks; keep under 12 words for social.
3. Score each: curiosity / clarity / emotion (1-5 each).
4. Deliver top 5 with variants (formal, casual, spicy).

## Output
Ranked hook table + why each works + the framework used.
